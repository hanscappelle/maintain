App name
Garageboek

Short description
Track vehicle maintenance, mileage, costs and notes in one place.

Full description
Keep a clear record of the vehicles you look after. Garageboek brings maintenance history, mileage, costs and notes together in your digital garage.

• Add cars, motorcycles and other vehicles.
• Record work, dates, odometer readings, notes and optional costs.
• See mileage history and costs at a glance.
• Keep important vehicle notes close at hand.
• Plan maintenance by date, mileage or both, and check upcoming work in the app.
• Archive vehicles without losing their history.
• Export a JSON backup and import it again when needed.

Your data is stored locally in the app, not in an online account. It is not backed up automatically: make regular backups to keep a copy. Planned maintenance is shown in the app; it does not send automatic notifications.
